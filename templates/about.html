About NexusDocs.ai

Understand the documents that cost you money

Most people click I Agree, renew a policy, or pay a bill without reading the file. The language is long, uneven, and easy to skim past until something goes wrong.

NexusDocs turns those documents into plain English so you can see what matters before you sign, renew, or pay. It is not a law firm, an insurer, or a billing department — and it does not replace your own judgment or a professional when the stakes are high.

What we do

You upload or paste a document. We give you a summary, flag the parts worth a second look, and let you ask follow-up questions about that same file. You can export a PDF to keep or share.

Three tools, one account:

  • TermsGuard: contracts, terms of service, privacy policies, and NDAs.
  • CoverClear: insurance policies — coverage, exclusions, and limits.
  • BillClear: medical bills and EOBs — line items and questions to ask before you pay.

How we work

  • Built for one job: explain this document, not chat about the internet.
  • Same checks each time: summary, flags, questions, optional PDF report.
  • Analysis, not storage: we process the text to answer you. We do not keep a permanent library of customer files. See our privacy policy.

Our mission

Help regular people and small businesses understand the fine print before it costs them time or money.

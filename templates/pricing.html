Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Free tier work?

You get 3 full document analyses per month. Each full analysis includes Summary + Risks, and you can ask unlimited follow-up questions on that document.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes. You can change your plan or switch to pay-as-you-go at any time.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. We do not store your documents. Everything is processed temporarily and deleted after analysis.

We accept all major credit cards via Stripe. No long-term contracts required.

How CoverClear Works

Built for long policies—not only the first page. We focus on coverage, limits, and exclusions, then explain them in plain English.

The policy is split into sections. CoverClear pulls the parts about coverage, limits, exclusions, and your question—so long documents aren’t judged only by the opening pages.
